About Semester 5

This page contains information about the Semester 5 program, learning outcomes, and aims.

Program Objectives

  • Provide advanced core knowledge required for professional practice.
  • Develop practical skills through projects and labs.
  • Prepare students for industry internships and final year projects.

Learning Outcomes

  1. Apply algorithms and data structures to solve medium-scale problems.
  2. Design and implement web systems using modern tools.
  3. Demonstrate teamwork and project management skills.
